## 🧩 What was done in Sprint 2

**Implemented:**

Work on the **Player** has been started.

Some issues occurred during development. Here are the main ones:

---

### 🛑 First problem

> Working with the API. For the Artist page, there was no way to obtain `tracksCount`, and when it seemed everything should work, Jamendo returned an error: _“no more than 50 values for a parameter in a single request”_.

**Solution:** after investigating the issue, an additional fetch request was implemented by splitting the data into chunks limited to 50 items and using `forkJoin` to combine the responses into one.

#### Result: `tracksCount` was successfully obtained for each artist’s album.

---

### 🛑 Second problem

> Waiting for review and approval of PR#89, PR#94, and PR#98. Colleagues were mostly busy with offline commitments. Meanwhile, three pull requests accumulated that could not be merged into `main` without at least one reviewer’s approval.

**Solution:** do what you must, and let the outcome be what it will be.

#### Result: all PRs were approved and merged into `main`.

---

### 🛑 Third problem

> A bug was discovered: after merging with `main`, the artist page stopped loading, while the album page worked correctly, even though they had similar implementations.

**Solution:** investigated console errors → during debugging it was concluded that the issue was in the route name → a careful inspection revealed a trailing space in the name. After removing it, the page started working again.

#### Result: the bug was fixed.
